# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DeepAgent contributors, maintainers, and community members must help keep participation open,
professional, and free from harassment. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome;
personal attacks, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption
are not.

Expected behavior includes:

- criticizing ideas and evidence rather than people;
- making review decisions from reproducible facts;
- respecting privacy and confidential security reports;
- accepting correction and repairing harm;
- using welcoming, accessible language.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, project-operated
community spaces, and public situations where someone represents DeepAgent.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub contact channel.
Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant,
depending on severity and pattern. Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse
themselves when appropriate.

Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
